# d-Stokvel - AI-Powered Decentralised Savings Platform

> Bridging traditional community finance and modern digital infrastructure.
>
> Built for the **African Youth AI Case Innovation Competition 2026**.

---

## The Problem

Stokvels move an estimated **R50 billion annually** through South Africa's informal economy, yet many groups still operate using handwritten records and interpersonal trust alone.

While these systems have supported communities for generations, they can create challenges such as:

* contribution disputes
* limited transparency
* difficulty assessing member reliability
* no portable financial reputation between groups
* little recognition from formal financial institutions

Many responsible savers therefore remain financially invisible despite years of disciplined participation.

---

## What d-Stokvel Does

d-Stokvel modernises the stokvel experience without stripping away its cultural identity or community-driven foundations.

The platform combines artificial intelligence, blockchain transparency and automated governance to strengthen trust rather than replace it.

Three core layers work together:

| Layer                       | What It Solves                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **AI Reliability Scoring**  | Members build a verifiable financial reputation from real behaviour without relying on traditional credit bureaus           |
| **Blockchain Transparency** | Contributions and payouts are recorded through tamper-resistant smart contracts, creating transparent and auditable records |
| **Governed Group Joining**  | Groups define their own trust thresholds while the platform enforces participation rules fairly and consistently            |

---

## Core Principles

d-Stokvel was designed around five principles:

* Preserve the social structure and identity of traditional stokvels.
* Increase trust without centralising power.
* Expand financial inclusion for underserved communities.
* Recognise alternative financial behaviour beyond conventional credit scoring.
* Create a pathway between informal and formal financial systems.

## User Tracks — How Members Onboard

The platform supports multiple onboarding journeys to ensure accessibility across different financial backgrounds.

### Track A - Banked Members

Members may provide banking records to establish an initial reputation profile based on historical financial behaviour and savings discipline.

---

### Track B - Existing Traditional Stokvel Members

Members can migrate participation histories from traditional stokvel systems using digitised records and document analysis technologies.

---

### Track C - New or Unbanked Members

No prior financial history is required.

New participants build reputation organically through positive participation, consistency and completed contribution cycles.

## Group Governance

d-Stokvel introduces automated governance mechanisms that help maintain fairness and sustainability within savings groups.

Examples include:

* configurable joining periods
* participation eligibility requirements
* affordability safeguards
* group size management
* transparent contribution tracking

Governance rules are enforced consistently across all participants, reducing disputes and administrative burden for organisers.
